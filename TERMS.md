# Terms of use

Copilot Credit Estimator is an internal evaluation tool. Estimates are
informational, are not binding offers and may differ from actual consumption.
Check current Microsoft licensing and pricing before making a purchasing decision.

Unconfirmed rates remain unknown. A known subtotal is not a complete price.
Native platform-advisor recommendations are provisional and should be checked
against the public Microsoft advisor.

Use only records you are authorised to access. Submission requires confirmation
in the model-driven app. Review decisions and rate-card approvals are restricted
to authorised users.
