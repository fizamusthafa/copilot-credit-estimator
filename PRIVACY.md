# Privacy

Copilot Credit Estimator stores estimates, use-case intake and review records in
the organisation's configured Microsoft Dataverse environment. Its tools run as
the signed-in user and remain subject to Dataverse permissions.

Microsoft 365 Copilot processes prompts and tool results under the organisation's
Microsoft service agreement and policies. See the
[Microsoft privacy statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).
Follow the organisation's retention and access policies. Do not enter passwords,
access tokens or personal information that is unnecessary for an estimate.

The reference-data synchronisation reads public pricing-reference files from
GitHub. It does not publish use cases, estimates or review records to GitHub.
Rate changes require approval before they are applied.
